# Numbers and words

## Number formats

| Thing | Format | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Currency | One currency per deck, set on the cover. Prefix, no space. Abbreviate ≥ 10,000 with K / M / B and one decimal max. | Rp389.9M · Rp1.96B · $248K · $41.2K |
| Exact money (pricing, contracts) | Full figure with thousands separators | IDR 17,000,000 / month |
| ROAS, multiples | Number + × (multiplication sign), one decimal | 4.1× · 11.8× · 3:1 for ratios of two things |
| Percent | One decimal max; none above 10% unless needed | 59.5% · 8% · 34.6% |
| Change | Signed with a real minus (−) and ▲/▼ badge; say the basis | ▲ 33% YoY · −17% vs normal weeks · +3.1% MoM |
| Percentage points | "pts" | +4.2 pts |
| Ranges | En dash, no spaces | 62–78% · Rp18–21.3M/wk |
| Approximate | "~" only for real estimates | ~Rp159M / month |
| Counts | Thousands separator; K above 10,000 | 1,940 · 24.8K · 312K |
| Periods | Month Year; ranges with en dash | Mar 2026 · Mar–Aug 2026 · H1 2026 · Q2 |
| IDs | P1–P5 priorities, T1–T10 actions | P1 · T7 |

Arrows are always the real glyphs → ← ↑ ↓ ▲ ▼ (never "->").

## Glossary (use these words, define once in an appendix)

ROAS (return on ad spend, platform-reported) · Blended ROAS (all revenue ÷ all spend) · CPL (cost per lead) · CPA (cost per acquisition) · GMV (gross merchandise value) · SRAB (sales-ready appointment booked) · CPAS (Meta collaborative ads) · Incremental (would not have happened without the spend) · Contribution margin (revenue − COGS − ad spend).

## Headline formula

Line 1: the fact, with a number if possible. Line 2 (blue): the turn — so what, or what next.

- "Spend up 58%. / Traffic down 17%."
- "60% of the budget sits in / the one channel we can't measure."
- "Real customers on a phone / beat the studio, 3 to 1."

Never a topic label ("Channel Performance"), never a question without an answer, never "It's not X, it's Y".

## Writing rules

- Sentences under 20 words. One idea each.
- Active, specific verbs: shift, cut, test, fix, scale.
- Name the source of every number. "Platform-reported" vs "business data" when both appear.
- Estimates are labelled "Estimated" or "Illustrative"; extrapolations get an asterisk note in the source line, never a red footnote.
- No hype, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- Indonesian clients: numbers stay in this format; copy may be Bahasa Indonesia, same rules.
